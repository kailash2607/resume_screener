Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer (Entry Level)

We are looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to join our data team.

- 0-2 years of experience in machine learning projects

Responsibilities:

- Clean and analyze large datasets
- Present insights to the team using dashboards and reports
